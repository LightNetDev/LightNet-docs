title: Glossary
description: Definitions of LightNet terms used in Ministry and Developer Documentation
---

This glossary defines LightNet terms used throughout the Ministry Documentation and
Developer Documentation. It is intended to give Ministries, Content Administrators, and
Site Administrators a shared vocabulary.

## Administration User Interface

An **Administration User Interface (Administration UI)** is a graphical interface for
managing **[Content](#content)** on a **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**.

## Author

An **Author** is a person or organization that created the **[Content](#content)** of a
**[Media Item](#media-item)**.

## Category

A **Category** is a grouping of related **[Media Items](#media-item)**. Categories help
people filter and organize **[Content](#content)** in a **[Media Library](#media-library)**.

## Content

**Content** is the material made available through a **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**,
such as videos, audio, images, and documents.

## Content Administrator

A **Content Administrator** is an editor who manages the **[Content](#content)** of a
**[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)** using an
**[Administration User Interface](#administration-user-interface)**.

## Content Language

A **Content Language** is the language in which a **[Media Item](#media-item)**'s
**[Content](#content)** is presented. For example, it is the language of the written
text in a document or the spoken language of a video.

## Contributor

A **Contributor** is a person who helps improve the open-source LightNet project through
feature proposals, bug reports, pull requests, or translation improvements.

## LightNet Site

A **LightNet Site** is an instance of LightNet operated by a **[Ministry](#ministry)** to
organize and distribute **[Content](#content)**.

## Media Collection

A **Media Collection** is a group of **[Media Items](#media-item)** arranged as a specific
sequence or set, such as the parts of a sermon series, audio recordings in a series, or
chapters in a video series.

## Media Distribution

**Media Distribution** is the process of delivering **[Content](#content)** to
**[Users](#user)** through a **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**.

## Media Item

A **Media Item** is the information that describes a single piece of **[Content](#content)**
distributed by a **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**. It can describe a video, audio,
image, or document, and provides access to the Content. The Content may be uploaded to
the LightNet Site or hosted elsewhere and linked to it. For example, a Ministry can
create a Media Item for each article provided as a PDF on its LightNet Site, or for each
sermon hosted on YouTube and curated on its LightNet Site.

## Media Library

A **Media Library** is a collection of **[Content](#content)** organized and made
available by a **[Ministry](#ministry)**. LightNet is a solution for creating and operating
Media Libraries. A **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)** is a Media Library.

## Media Type

A **Media Type** describes the kind of **[Content](#content)** represented by a
**[Media Item](#media-item)**. Ministries define the Media Types used by their
**[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**, choosing names that match the language they use for
their media.

## MediaWorks

[MediaWorks](https://www.om.org/eng/mediaworks) is a ministry of Operation Mobilisation
(OM) that sponsors the LightNet open-source project.

MediaWorks equips local ministries around the globe with relevant print and digital media
that helps them share the gospel and strengthen believers in their communities.

## Ministry

A **Ministry** is a Christian organization that serves a local community.

## Site Administrator

A **Site Administrator** is responsible for designing, building, deploying, and
operating one or more **[LightNet Sites](#lightnet-site)**.
The [Developer Documentation](/start-here/getting-started) provides technical guidance
for Site Administrators.

## Site Language

A **Site Language** is the language of the user interface, including button labels,
menus, and page text.

## User

A **User** is anyone who consumes **[Content](#content)** via a **[LightNet Site](#lightnet-site)**.
