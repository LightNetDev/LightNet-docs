GitHub stores the code and information that describes the content on your
[LightNet site](/ministry/resources/glossary/#lightnet-site). Content administrators
also use their GitHub accounts to sign in to the
[Administration UI](/ministry/resources/glossary/#administration-user-interface).

This guide helps your ministry create a GitHub account, set up an organization,
and give your [site administrator](/ministry/resources/glossary/#site-administrator)
access to complete the technical setup.

## Before you begin

GitHub has two types of accounts you will use:

- A personal account identifies one person. Each person signs in with their own
  account.
- An organization is a shared workspace for your ministry's site and the people
  who manage it. For example, a ministry called Hope Media might choose the
  organization name “hope-media”.

Choose someone at your ministry to create the organization and remain an owner.
Ask your site administrator for their GitHub username so you can invite them later.
If your ministry already has a GitHub organization, agree with its owners whether
to use it for this project, then go to the invitation step.

## Create your account and organization

<Steps>

1. Create a personal account

   Open [GitHub signup](https://github.com/signup), follow the prompts, and verify
   your email address. If you already have a personal account, sign in to it.

2. Create the ministry's organization

   While signed in, open [Create a GitHub organization](https://github.com/account/organizations/new) and create a **free** GitHub organisation.

   Choose an organization name that represents your ministry and enter a contact
   email address the ministry can continue to access. Complete the remaining
   prompts. If GitHub offers to invite members during setup, skip that step;
   you will invite your site administrator below.

</Steps>

## Invite your site administrator

The Owner role gives someone full administrative access to the organization.
For this setup, you will grant that role to your site administrator so they can
configure the services your site needs. Keep a ministry representative as an owner
as well, so the ministry retains access and can manage who helps with the site.
See [GitHub's explanation of organization roles](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-peoples-access-to-your-organization-with-roles/roles-in-an-organization).

<Steps>

1. Open your organization

   Click your profile picture in GitHub, select Organizations, then select your
   ministry's organization.

2. Send an invitation

   Open the People tab and select Invite member. Search for your site
   administrator's GitHub username, select the matching account, and click Invite.
   Under Role in the organization, choose Owner, then select Send invitation.

3. Confirm that the invitation was accepted

   Ask your site administrator to accept the invitation. Return to the People
   tab and confirm that they appear as an owner. Invitations expire after seven
   days; if needed, follow
   [GitHub's invitation instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-membership-in-your-organization/inviting-users-to-join-your-organization)
   to retry an expired invitation.

</Steps>
