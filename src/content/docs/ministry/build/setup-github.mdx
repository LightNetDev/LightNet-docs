GitHub is an online service that stores the code and content information for your
[LightNet site](/ministry/resources/glossary/#lightnet-site). Content administrators
also use their GitHub accounts to sign in to the
[Administration UI](/ministry/resources/glossary/#administration-user-interface).

In this guide, you will create a shared space for your ministry on GitHub and
invite your [site administrator](/ministry/resources/glossary/#site-administrator)
to complete the technical setup. You do not need to know how to write code to
follow these steps.

## Before you begin

You will use two things on GitHub:

- A personal account is your own sign-in. Each person uses their own account,
  including your site administrator.
- An organization is a shared space for your ministry's site. You use your
  personal account to create it and decide who else can access it. The
  organization does not have a separate password.

Choose a ministry representative to follow this guide and remain responsible for
the organization. Have these details ready:

- An email address you can access to verify your personal account.
- A contact email address for the organization that your ministry can continue
  to access.
- Your site administrator's GitHub username (the name that identifies their
  account), so you can invite the right person.

If your ministry already has a GitHub organization, ask one of its owners whether
you can use it for this project. If they agree, ask them to follow
[Invite your site administrator](#invite-your-site-administrator) below.

## Create your account and organization

<Steps>

1. Create a personal account

   If you already have a personal GitHub account, sign in to it and continue to
   the next step.

   Otherwise, open [GitHub signup](https://github.com/signup) and follow the
   prompts to choose a username and create your account. Check your email and
   complete GitHub's email verification.

2. Create the ministry's organization

   While signed in, open
   [Create a GitHub organization](https://github.com/account/organizations/new).
   Choose the GitHub Free plan. See
   [GitHub's plan information](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/learning-about-github/githubs-plans)
   if you need to compare the options.

   Enter a name that represents your ministry. For example, Hope Media might
   choose “hope-media”. If the name is already taken, try a variation such as
   “hope-media-ministry”. Enter the ministry's contact email address and follow
   the remaining prompts.

   If GitHub offers to invite people during setup, skip that step. You will invite
   your site administrator in the next section.

3. Save the organization link

   Open your new organization's page and save its web address so you can find it
   again. Share this link with your site administrator.

   Your personal account is now an owner of the organization. Keep access to
   this account so your ministry can continue to manage the organization.

</Steps>

## Invite your site administrator

An owner can manage the organization's settings and decide who has access.
You will give your site administrator the Owner role so they can complete the
technical setup. Keep the ministry representative as an owner too, so the
ministry retains control. See
[GitHub's explanation of organization roles](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-peoples-access-to-your-organization-with-roles/roles-in-an-organization).

<Steps>

1. Open your organization

   In GitHub, click your profile picture at the top right, select
   **Organizations**, then select your ministry's organization.

2. Send an invitation

   Open the **People** tab and select **Invite member**. Search for your site
   administrator's GitHub username. Check that you have the right account, then
   select **Invite**.

   Under **Role in the organization**, choose Owner, then select
   **Send invitation**.

3. Confirm that the invitation was accepted

   Ask your site administrator to open the invitation email from GitHub and
   accept it. Return to the People tab and check that their account appears with
   the Owner role.

   Invitations expire after seven days. If they miss the email or the invitation
   expires, use
   [GitHub's invitation instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-membership-in-your-organization/inviting-users-to-join-your-organization)
   to send it again.

</Steps>

## When you are finished

Check that both the ministry representative and the site administrator appear
as owners on the organization's People tab. Let your site administrator know
that access is ready; they can now continue with the technical setup.
