Cloudflare is an online service that hosts your
[LightNet site](/ministry/resources/glossary/#lightnet-site), making it available
to visitors on the internet. You can also use it to buy a domain name, such as
“hope-media.org”, for your site's web address.

In this guide, you will create an account for your ministry and invite your
[site administrator](/ministry/resources/glossary/#site-administrator) to complete
the technical setup. You do not need to know how to write code to follow these
steps.

## Before you begin

Choose a ministry representative to create the account and keep access to it.
Have these details ready:

- An email address for the representative that your ministry can continue to
  access.
- The email address your site administrator uses to sign in to Cloudflare.

Each person uses their own sign-in. You will invite your site administrator to
access the ministry's account so they can work on your site.

If your ministry already has a Cloudflare account, agree with the person who
manages it whether to use it for this project. If they agree, ask them to follow
[Invite your site administrator](#invite-your-site-administrator) below.

## Create your account

<Steps>

1. Sign up for Cloudflare

   Open [Cloudflare signup](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up) and create a
   free account using your email address and a password.

   You can also use Continue with GitHub to sign up with your
   [GitHub account](/ministry/build/setup-github/). Cloudflare uses your GitHub
   account's primary email address for this option.

2. Verify your email address

   Check your inbox for an email from Cloudflare and follow its verification
   link. You need a verified email address before you can invite your site
   administrator.

   <Aside type="note" title="Protect your account">

   We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) for extra
   security. This adds a second check when you sign in, such as a code from an
   app on your phone. Follow
   [Cloudflare's guide to setting up two-factor authentication](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/user-profiles/2fa/).
   </Aside>

</Steps>

## Invite your site administrator

The Administrator role lets your site administrator configure hosting and change
subscriptions. It does not let them manage account members or the billing
profile. Keep the ministry representative's Super Administrator access so the
ministry can continue to manage who has access. See
[Cloudflare's explanation of account roles](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/).

<Steps>

1. Open the member list

   Sign in to the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) and select
   your ministry's account. In the left sidebar, open **Manage account**, then
   **Members**.

2. Enter your site administrator's email address

   Select **Invite members**. Under **Add email addresses**, enter the email
   address your site administrator uses for Cloudflare.

3. Choose their access and send the invitation

   Under **Add permission policies**, choose the entire account as the scope
   (the area they can access) and select the Administrator role. Select
   **Create policy**, then Invite members.

4. Confirm that the invitation was accepted

   Ask your site administrator to accept Cloudflare's invitation email. Return
   to Members and check that their status has changed from Pending to Active.

   If they cannot find the email, open the menu beside their pending invitation
   and select **Resend invite**. See
   [Cloudflare's invitation instructions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/manage-members/manage/)
   for help.

</Steps>

## When you are finished

Check that the ministry representative still has Super Administrator access and
the site administrator has Administrator access. Let your site administrator
know the account is ready so they can set up hosting and help connect your
domain name. Agree on any purchases or paid subscriptions with them before
proceeding.
